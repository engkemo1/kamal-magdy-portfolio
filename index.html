Chapter 03 — The Crucible

Defining Moments

ODC FLUTTER HACKATHON #1

Overcoming the Impossible

In 2022, while balancing my final university exams, I saw a post on LinkedIn: The ODC Flutter Hackathon.

I entered a room full of experienced developers. The pressure was immense. How could I compete with so many pros? I remember telling myself: "Nothing is impossible. Believe in Allah and do your best."

01
The Course Built a full Learning Management System in 4 days. Figma design, complex APIs, and Clean Architecture — and stayed among the final 30 participants.
02
The Game The final challenge: "Build a game in 4 hours." Under heavy pressure, I planned, designed and coded a Flutter Color Game — one of the best in the room.

Seven days later, I got the call: ranked 4th in the Hackathon. That led to a 6-month journey with ODC, proving that passion and hard work can conquer any obstacle.

How I engineer

01

The server is the authority

Prices, permissions and order states are decided on the backend. The app never gets to say what something costs or that a payment succeeded.

02

Offline when it matters

A clinic can't stop because the internet did. Local servers, LAN discovery and encrypted backups are design choices, not afterthoughts.

03

One codebase, many surfaces

Shared models and packages across mobile, web and desktop, so a rule changes once and every screen follows.

04

Tested where it breaks

Tests go where the money and the data are: pricing, state transitions, permissions and webhooks.
